## Free AI for coding

Best order to try, cheapest effort first:

1. **Cline** (VS Code extension or Cline Desktop) → pick a model marked "(free)": DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Kimi K3 (Desktop), GLM-5.3-Flash, Laguna S 2.1, Solar Pro 4.
2. **Freebuff** → `npm i -g freebuff`, 100 free credits a day (6 h of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, 5 h of GPT-6 Luna). Ad-supported.
3. **OpenCode** + a free key → Token Harbor (`deepseek-v4.1-flash:free`, `qwen3.8-flash:free`, `mimo-v2.6-flash:free`), Google AI Studio, or OpenRouter `:free`. Config snippet in [FREE-ACCESS.md](./FREE-ACCESS.md#how-to-use-all-of-this-opencode--any-openai-compatible-key).
4. **Kilo Code** → `kilo-auto/free` picks a free model for you (200 req/h without an account).
5. **Antigravity** (Google) → free plan with Gemini 3.8 Flash and weekly agent limits.
6. **Local** → Ollama + Qwen3.8-Flash-Next or Laguna XS 2.1 — unlimited and private, slower.

Rule: free models are fine for 90% of student work (assignments, projects, learning). Save any paid or trial credit for the one hard bug.

## Don't get scammed

- ⛔ **aerolink.lat** and **lumosel.vip** sell "cheap Claude Opus/Sonnet" but actually serve **Qwen** and **DeepSeek**, with fake token counters. They call themselves Chinese grey-market proxies. Many similar Chinese credit routers do the same. Details and a 3-step check: [FREE-ACCESS.md](./FREE-ACCESS.md#chinese-credit-routers-community-reported--unverified).
- ⛔ **ZCode** uploaded users' whole repos (with Git history and old secrets). See [NEWS.md](./NEWS.md).
- ⛔ Cracked keys, "keygen/activator" repos, shared-account sites, and anything asking for your real Claude/ChatGPT password. These steal accounts or install malware.
- ⚠️ Free and stealth models (OpenRouter stealth, TokenRa, Muse "contributor", Gemini free tier) may train on your prompts. Never paste API keys, passwords, or private data.
- ⚠️ "$100 free credits" gateways usually shrink the offer over time and charge high prices after. Treat the credit as a trial, not a plan.

## Spend fewer tokens

Free tiers run out because of wasted context, not because you asked too much. Short version:

1. **New task, new chat.** Long chats resend everything every turn.
2. **Paste only the relevant part** of a file or error, not the whole thing.
3. **Use the small model first** (Flash / Luna / Haiku / V4.1 Flash); switch up only when it fails.
4. **Plan, then build** — ask for a plan in one message, approve it, then implement.
5. In coding agents: `/clear` between tasks, `/compact` at milestones, keep a short project notes file (`CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md`).

Full guide with per-app tips: [USAGE.md](./USAGE.md#token-efficiency-make-free-limits-last).
